## 📌 About the Project

How can data help a business understand its performance, identify trends, and make better decisions?

**Global Superstore Sales Analysis** is an interactive Power BI project designed to explore retail business performance through data visualization and analysis.

The project transforms sales data into meaningful insights by examining sales trends, profitability, customer segments, product performance, regional differences, shipping costs, and returned orders.

With five report pages, the dashboard provides different perspectives on business performance, from detailed analysis to an executive-level overview.

**The goal:** Make business data easier to understand, explore, and interpret.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 📊 Analyze sales and profit performance.
- 🌍 Compare performance across regions and markets.
- 🛍️ Identify top-performing products and categories.
- 👥 Explore sales contributions from customer segments.
- 💰 Investigate profitability and discount patterns.
- 🚚 Analyze shipping costs and returned orders.
- 📈 Present business metrics through interactive visualizations.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard supports several useful business observations:

- **Regional performance:** Compare sales across regions to understand where revenue is concentrated.
- **Product performance:** Identify products contributing the most to total sales.
- **Profitability:** Examine how profit varies across regions, categories, and customer segments.
- **Discount analysis:** Investigate whether higher discount levels are associated with lower profitability.
- **Returns and shipping:** Explore return patterns and shipping costs to identify areas that may require additional attention.

These observations can help guide further analysis and support data-informed business decisions.
